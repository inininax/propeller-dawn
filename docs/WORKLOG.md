### Bugs found by tests and fixed during this session

1. Scene-restart stale references: Title/Pause/ShipSelect/Settings accumulated destroyed buttons across restarts → crash (`drawImage` of null) on Title revisit. Fixed by resetting collections in `create()`.
2. Ember Crown was invulnerable via center hits during thruster phase — correct per design, but debug kill path needed part-aware targeting; real gameplay unaffected.
3. Wave warp could complete a stage before the final boss spawned (completion checked `boss === null`). Added `finalBossSpawned` gate.
4. `rearDash` path ignored spawn origin (enemies teleported). Rewritten relative to spawn point; Stage 2 Razor Swifts now enter from below the play area as designed.
5. Save migration salvaged nothing from partially-corrupt hiscores; now sanitizes per-key.

Stage 2 normal play + Solbreaker-style boss window: **avg 85 FPS, min 63 FPS**, peak 39 simultaneous enemy bullets (pool cap 620). Full numbers in `docs/TEST_REPORT.md`.
